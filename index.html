What we do

Our Services

Full-service electrical for homes and businesses across Southwest Florida — installed, repaired, and upgraded by electricians who take the time to get it right.

Residential Electrical

Outlets, switches, fixtures, ceiling fans, rewiring, and troubleshooting for your home — done cleanly and explained clearly.

Commercial Electrical

Tenant build-outs, restaurants, offices, and retail. Code-compliant work delivered on schedule with minimal disruption.

Panel & Service Upgrades

Replace outdated or undersized panels, add circuits, and upgrade service to handle today’s loads safely.

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home and commercial charging stations, sized and installed correctly with any required panel work.

Lighting Design & Install

Recessed, pendant, LED strip, landscape, and security lighting that makes your space look and work better.

Dock & Marine Power
